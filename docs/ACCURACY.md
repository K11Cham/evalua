## Read this first

- The fake transcripts and the parser were written by the same author, so layouts the generator does not produce are not measured here. Real documents will do worse than these numbers, especially handwritten, stamped-over, or very low-resolution ones.
- “Subject matched” compares against one reasonable expected subject per course. Some are judgement calls (for example, how to classify a combined history-geography course).
- “US letter grade” checks that the cited conversion table was applied correctly. It does not show that the table is the right policy for your district.
- English names come from dictionaries, not a translator, so wording differs from a human translation even when the subject is right.
